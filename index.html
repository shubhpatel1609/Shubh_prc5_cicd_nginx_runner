01 — ABOUT

A little about how I work.

I spend most of my time in the gap between what a system can do and what a person actually needs. That usually means fewer screens, not more — cutting until only the necessary decisions are left.

Before design, I studied cognitive science, which mostly taught me that people are far better at recognizing than recalling. Every interface I build tries to respect that.
